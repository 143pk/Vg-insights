15. Two identical bodies are made of a material for which the heat capacity increases with temperature. One of these is at 100°C, while the other one is at 0°C. If the two bodies are brought into contact, then assuming no heat loss, the final common temperature is
(A) 50°C
(B) More than 50°C
(C) Less than 50°C but greater than 0°C
(D) 0°C
Solution: (B)
Loss of heat by hot body = Gain of heat by cold body.
Since heat capacity increases with temperature, the hotter body has a higher average heat capacity over the range. Hence the temperature drop of the hot body will be less than the temperature rise of the cold body, resulting in a final equilibrium temperature greater than 50°C.
